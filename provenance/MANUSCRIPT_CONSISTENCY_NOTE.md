# Manuscript consistency note

The validated disagreement decomposition is:

- 29 evidence-majority 3--1 splits;
- 3 label-majority 1--3 splits; and
- 7 two-to-two splits.

These categories sum to the 39 contrasts with cross-model disagreement. A
manuscript statement reporting “29 three-to-one splits” should specify that the
29 are evidence-majority splits. The total number of three-to-one splits in
either direction is 32.
