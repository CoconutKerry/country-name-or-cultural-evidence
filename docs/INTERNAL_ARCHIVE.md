# Internal archive

The clean repository is accompanied by a separate internal archive containing
the original authoritative result packages, source-only handoff packages, and
paper source snapshot. These originals are preserved byte-for-byte and listed
in `provenance/ORIGINAL_ARTIFACTS.csv` with SHA-256 values.

The internal archive should remain private. Failed runs and obsolete historical
outputs are intentionally excluded from the clean repository; they may be kept
separately if needed for project history, but they are not scientific inputs to
the reported analysis.
